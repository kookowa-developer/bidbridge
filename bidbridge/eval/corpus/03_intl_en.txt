INVITATION FOR BIDS
Procurement of Medical Equipment

1. Bids must be delivered on or before 20 January 2027 at 15:00 (Tashkent time) together with a Bid Security in the amount of USD 120,000.
2. Bidders must have a minimum annual turnover of USD 2,000,000 in each of the last three years.
3. Bidders must have at least 5 years of experience in the supply of similar equipment.
4. Bidders must submit a valid manufacturer's authorization, a certificate of incorporation, a tax clearance certificate and a copy of the license.
5. Late bids will be rejected.
